## Context

What is the issue we're seeing that motivates this decision or change? Describe the
forces at play: technical constraints, project scope, timeline, cost, team size, and
any relevant background. State the problem neutrally — this section should read the same
regardless of which option is ultimately chosen.

## Decision

The change we're actually proposing or making. State it clearly and in the active voice
("We will …"). Include enough specificity that a reader can tell what was decided without
guessing.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. List both the positive outcomes and the costs,
risks, or follow-up work this decision creates. Be honest about trade-offs.

per alternative helps a future reader understand the decision space.
